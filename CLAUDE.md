## Assets

- `assets/ambientcg/` holds CC0 PBR texture sets (color, normal, roughness; `Fence006` has opacity in `color.png` alpha): grass, dry ground, dirt, asphalt, concrete, corrugated steel, metal plates, solar cells, chain-link. Re-import with `python tools/import_textures.py`, then `--import`, then `python tools/import_textures.py --fix-imports` (headless imports can't detect 3D use: mipmaps, VRAM compression, normal-map flag), then `--import` again.
- `assets/quaternius/`: the Quaternius Universal Animation Library 1 and 2 (CC0, `UAL*_License.txt`). The in-place Godot GLBs sit in `source/` (a `.gdignore` folder: not imported). `tools/retarget/retarget_ual.tscn` (run headless) bakes the clips listed in its `CLIPS` onto the Kenney rig into `ual_kenney.res`: rotations in each rig's model space with a rest-direction alignment, hips translation scaled by hip height. Add a clip there (pick names from the probe or quaternius.com/animviewer.html), rerun, and commit the .res. The downloaded zips in `assets/` are git-ignored.
- `assets/kenney/` holds CC0 Kenney packs (each folder keeps its `License.txt`): `suburban` (houses, paths), `commercial` (City Kit Commercial: store buildings a..n), `cars`, `characters` (one animated humanoid FBX, idle/run/jump clips, base skins), `nature` (trees, bushes, flowers), `particles`. `assets/audio/` is built by `tools/generate_sfx.py --kenney <unzipped packs>`: synthesized guns, explosions, engine/turbine/fire/birds loops, barks, and boss babble (.wav), plus Kenney Impact/Interface/Sci-fi Sounds and Music Jingles picks (.ogg). Files are `<cue>_<n>` (n = random variants). `python tools/generate_music.py` (numpy + scipy + soundfile) writes the four looping `music_<track>.ogg` background tracks (calm, assault, build, wave); OGG is written in blocks because one large Vorbis write crashes libsndfile.
- Generated from those, reproducible with Python + Pillow (commit the outputs):
  - `python tools/generate_skins.py`: faction outfit skins (outfits x 5 skin tones; `TONE_OVERRIDES` for pale reply guys and white-haired grandmas) by recoloring atlas regions of `skaterMaleA`. Add an outfit there, then use its name as `Enemy.outfit` / `CharacterModel.create()`.
  - `python tools/generate_palettes.py`: roof-color variants of the suburban palette.
  - `python tools/prepare_particles.py`: `*_alpha.png` particle textures (white, alpha from brightness). The pack's own PNGs are opaque black backgrounds; always use the alpha versions.

## Conventions

- **GDScript uses tabs** (overrides the global 2-space rule). Godot's editor and style guide use tabs, and mixing tabs and spaces is a parse error. See `.editorconfig`.
- Static typing everywhere. Call duck-typed methods with `node.call(&"apply_damage", ...)` after `has_method`.
- Input actions are registered in code (`scripts/autoload/game.gd`), not in `project.godot`.
- Anything damageable implements `apply_damage(amount: float, from: Vector3, kind: StringName)`. `from` is the **attacker's position** (riot shields and debris direction depend on it). Kinds: `bullet`, `explosive`, `fire`, `impact`, `bite`, `taser`, `melee`, `shockwave`, `emp`. Riot shields don't block `explosive` or `fire`. Things that can be shoved implement `apply_knockback(impulse)`.
- Physics layers: 1 world, 2 player, 3 vehicles, 4 debris, 5 destructible, 6 enemies, 7 bodies (ragdolls; bits 1/2/4/8/16/32/64, constants in `Game`). Ragdolls collide only with world and props, so cars drive over bodies (a small suspension thump and camera shake on a kill) instead of being launched by them. `@tool` scripts can't use the `Game` autoload, so they hardcode the numbers with a comment.
- Adding bodies inside physics callbacks is not allowed: defer (see `Destructible.shatter`).
- Parameters that may receive freed nodes (timer/tween callbacks, target checks) are `Variant` and check `is_instance_valid`. A typed `Node3D` parameter errors on a freed object.
- A `const` can't hold class references (`{"guard": SecurityGuard}`): use `static var`.
- Gameplay timers use game time (delta countdowns), never `Time.get_ticks_msec()`: tests run at `--fixed-fps` and games pause.
- Subclass `_init()` runs after the parent's, so subclasses override stats there (verified in `bosses_test`).

## Architecture

- `Game` (autoload): cash, objective, current `DistrictState`, input map, `reset()` per level load, `set_info(key, text)` for HUD lines ("boss", "wave", "core", "notice", and the "build"/"bribe"/"shop" key menus), `set_checklist(key, title, rows)` ("sites", "deeds"), `show_banner()`, `shake()`, `bribes` (pending one-shot favors: `has_bribe` / `consume_bribe`). Also `notify(text)` (HUD toast), `tip(key, text)` (one-time tip, per session; `show_tips` setting), `count(stat)` / `stat()` for the end screen, and settings (`mouse_sensitivity`, `fullscreen`, `show_tips`; section `game` of `user://settings.cfg`).
- `Sfx` (autoload): `play(cue, at)` positional one-shots from a pool (max 4 voices per cue), `ui(cue)` for 2D, `loop(host, cue)` / `loop_2d()` for engines, turbines, fires, birds, and `music(track)` (crossfades the looping background track; "" fades out; jingles on the Music bus duck it). The level picks the track per phase (`_announce_phase`), the title plays "calm". Buses Master/Music/SFX/UI/Ambience created in code; volumes in section `audio`.
- Replay structure: `IntroOverlay` (skippable captions; the level adds it when `Game.pending_intro`, set by the title's Play). Difficulty (`Game.difficulty` 0-2, settings `game/difficulty`): `damage_taken_scale()` (player), `enemy_health_scale()` (non-boss hostiles, in `Enemy._ready`), `wave_size_scale()` (WaveSpawner counts, bosses stay one). `SaveGame` (user://save.json; any scene under res://tests/ uses `TEST_PATH` instead): `level.save_checkpoint()` at the defense start and after each cleared wave, cleared on WON/LOST; the title shows "Continue (wave N of 5)" and sets `Game.pending_save`; `level._resume()` removes the three sites (keeping the Felsa lot position in `_lot_override`), runs `start_defense()`, and restores cash, district, bribes, stats, core health, the wave, and the structures (`BuildController.place(..., no_cost)`). After each cleared wave the HUD shows a summary card (`Hud.show_wave_summary()`: hostiles stopped, core damage, cash earned, structures lost, next wave via `WaveSpawner.describe_wave()`).
- Menus (`scripts/ui/`, styled by `UiTheme`): `title.tscn` is the main scene (Play / Settings / Controls / Quit). The level adds a `PauseMenu` (Esc or P; pauses the tree, frees the mouse; restart, quit to title), an `EndScreen` (win/lose stats 3 s after WON/LOST; the world keeps running), and a `TipDirector` (a first-minute tutorial, then one-time tips near props, enemy types, and bosses, and at phase changes). `SettingsPanel` and `ControlsPanel` are shared by the title and pause menus.
- HUD (`hud.gd`, `HudBar`, `HudIcons`, `Minimap`): circular heading-up minimap top-left (roads and footprints from `NeighborhoodBuilder.footprints()`, site outlines by status, POIs H/P/M/W/G/R/C, units, a yellow marker for `level.guidance_point()`), [M] full north-up map with legend, icon meters, big health bar with damage flash and low-health vignette, turbo bar while driving, weapon/ammo panel. Redraws are throttled (the minimap at 12 Hz); drawing it every frame cost ~20 FPS.
- HUD layout: objective (2 lines max) and status lines (boss, wave, core, notice) top-center, 600 px wide; a right column with cash, checklists (`Game.set_checklist(key, title, rows)`, rows `[text, &"todo"|&"done"|&"alert"|&"info"]`; the level sends "sites" and "deeds", cleared on phase change; `Hud.checklist_rows(key)` for tests), then toasts; key menus ("build", "bribe", "shop" info lines) bottom-center above the prompt.
- Speech bubbles: `HudOverlay.as_bubble(label, boss)` hides a speech Label3D (group `speech`) and the overlay draws its text in a rounded bubble with a tail at the label's position (fades by 45 m; bosses get a red border). `Enemy.speak`, Elmo, Elmo's truck, Crapya, and the paint-job neighbor use it; the label still carries the text and stacking height.
- Signs: `Models.fit_label(label, board_size)` sizes a Label3D's text to fit inside its board (measured with its font) and makes it front-only; every board sign uses it.
- AI traffic (`FelsaCar.in_traffic_mode()`: the supply van, cargo trucks, the tourist RV, a non-pursuing police cruiser, and dormant site Cyberdouches; `obeys_traffic`): capped at `traffic_speed`, three forward rays (`SENSE_MASK`: player, vehicles, units) stop it for anything ahead, it slows into turns, and after ~3 s blocked it dodges around. In traffic mode the bumper only nudges people (no damage). `motor_cue`: Cyberdouches whine (`ev_loop`); vans and cruisers use `engine_loop`.
- Stealth (`Enemy._watch_for_player`, while `is_dormant()` and `_watches()`: guards (`uses_cover`) and site guard dogs): each think tick a watcher that sees the player (view cone `_view_cos()` 0.5, i.e. 60 degrees each side, range `_view_range()` 18 m; dogs 8 m all around; within 2 m always; `_can_see` line of sight) while the player is inside its site's compound (`DatacenterSite.contains(point, 1.0)`) or aiming a gun within 12 m (`Player.is_threatening()`) gains `suspicion` at 1/1.2 s up close to 1/5 s at the edge of range, times `Player.stealth_rate()` (crouched 0.45, sprinting 1.4, in a car 2.0, x1.5 when threatening); range scales by `Player.stealth_visibility()` (crouched 0.55, sprinting 1.2, car 1.5). Unseen it fades 0.12/s. Over 0.35 the unit comes to look (`investigate`) and says so; at 1 it calls `raise_alarm(site, label, seen_by)` ("A security guard spotted you"). `stand_down()` clears it. HUD: `HudOverlay._draw_suspicion` draws a "?" and a yellow-to-red meter (`suspicious_units()`). Player crouch: [C] (`crouch` action; `descend` shares the key in the drone), `CROUCH_SPEED` 2.3, stance `crouch_idle` / `crouch_walk` (new retargeted clip), sprinting or jumping stands up; the kit line says SNEAKING.
- Guard tactics (`SecurityGuard._run_tactics`, after each think tick; `uses_cover`, off for `SentryTurret` and `HoloClone`): against the player on foot beyond `CLOSE_QUARTERS` (5 m), a guard looks for cover within `cover_search` (8 m: rays from the player's eye past the guard find obstacles in `COVER_MASK` (world, destructibles); a candidate is the navmesh spot 1 m behind a hit that stays hidden 0.5 m to either side (`_covered`) and has a clear peek spot 1.6-2.6 m to the side), hides there (holds fire), then PEEKs out to shoot for ~2 s (stepping toward the player if the peek spot has no shot) and ducks back. In `units_test` a guard behind a wall is hidden ~40% of the time and lands about half the hits it would in the open. Settled in cover it crouches (`_stance()` -> `CharacterModel.set_stance(&"crouch_idle")`), and arriving always starts a fresh hide (1-1.6 s).
- Police shield wall (`Police._hold_the_line`): two or more officers with shields up targeting the player on foot take slots in a line (`LINE_SPACING` 1.3 m) across the approach, stopping at taser range; each keeps facing the player while walking (`Enemy._look_while_moving()`), and anyone more than `LINE_SLACK` ahead of the line's average slows (`Enemy.speed_scale`). `in_line` is public (tests). With another guard on the player, one guard at a time may FLANK: a navmesh spot swung ~75 degrees around the player, holding fire until it gets there. `tactic` is public (tests). Guards also target the player's recon drone.
- Tamed dogs are bodyguards (`Dog._defending`): they only target hostiles whose target is the player, the player's car, or the dog, within 20 m of the player.
- Speech bubbles are hidden when a wall or prop is between the camera and the speaker (overlay raycast, mask world + destructibles).
- Jumps: `CharacterModel.set_airborne()` blends a full-body `air` layer to `jump_loop`; the player plays the takeoff once, holds the air pose after 0.25 s, and a landing clip after longer falls. While driving, the HUD shows a car health bar ("CAR ON FIRE: GET OUT" under 15%).
- DUECE Hardware stands: one row centered on the door, 1.9 m apart, with colliders and tags above the tables.
- Car rams: props always take ram damage, but people and vehicles only from a car that is driven or was driven in the last 4 s (`rams_units()`): parked cars shoved by traffic used to hit the patrol car and neighbors, raising the police alarm on the player. Skid marks live under a "SkidMarks" node in the level (`skid_mark_count()`).
- Speech (`Enemy.speak`): at most `MAX_TALKERS` (3) non-boss bubbles at once, only within `TALK_RANGE` (35 m) of the camera; the nearest win (a farther talker is silenced) and bubbles within 3 m stack upward. Bosses always talk.
- Wave failsafe (`WaveSpawner._check_stuck`, after `rush_after`): a counted unit with no target that stays within 1.5 m for `stuck_seconds` is snapped to the navmesh and re-sent (`Enemy.renavigate`), and on a second strike withdraws (`Enemy.give_up`, counts as defeated). With 3 or fewer left, `guidance_point()` points at the nearest (`WaveSpawner.nearest_remaining`).
- HUD clarity (`Hud` adds both): `HudOverlay` draws world-anchored info over the view: the objective marker (from `level.guidance_point()`, pinned to the screen edge with its distance), health bars and a "!" over nearby hostiles that are hurt or fighting (bosses use the HUD bar), floating damage numbers (`Player.damage_dealt`), red hit-direction arcs (`Player.hurt_from`), and title cards (`Game.show_banner(title, subtitle)`; the level announces phases and waves). `CameraFx` (group `camera_fx`) works on the current camera: FOV kick on sprint and turbo, shake via `Game.shake(at, strength)` (explosions), and dust motes that thin as smog clears. The [E]/[F] prompt is a badge; the kit line shows `Allies xN` (`Hud.ally_count()`).
- Residents (`Resident.set_role()` before add_child): walker, jogger, dog_walker (a pet `Dog` with `pet_owner`, group `pets`), kid, gardener, mail_carrier, busker (by the market). Each role has its own outfit, props, pace, and lines.
- Lost Canadians: the level sends up to `tourist_groups` `TouristRV`s (first after `tourist_first` s, then every `tourist_interval` s, during ACTIVISM/ASSAULT/BUILD; `spawn_tourists()` for tests). The RV parks at a `tourist_stops` point and unloads 2-3 `Canuck`s (it parks on the shoulder and drives off once they join) (group `tourists`, sometimes with a Mountie). [E] on any of them makes the whole group join (group `allies`, +0.03 trust, stat `canadians`); they follow the player and fight with hockey sticks, then go home when the next defense wave clears (`_send_canadians_home`). During BUILD/WAVE, with the player more than `post_leash` (30 m) from the core, they hold the core instead (`level.ally_post()`, `Canuck._defense_post()`) and only engage hostiles within `post_radius` (14 m) of it: chasing farther got every recruit killed from wave 3 on (each death costs 0.02 trust). During a wave with a cut fence breach (and in BUILD once one is announced) the post is just inside the breach (`level.ally_post()`, `_breach_hold`). Below `fall_back_share` (35%) health a posted Canadian falls back to the core (`falling_back`), fights only within 4 m, and recovers `recover_rate` hp/s until `rejoin_share`. Mounties carry a service revolver (13 m hitscan, `revolver_damage` 12, `pistol_aim` pose): each group's ranged member. `balance_sim -- canadians=3` (now with a "lost" structures column): survival unchanged (4/5, the turrets already hold), but structures lost in breach waves drop from roughly 4-12 to 0-3 per wave in most runs. No new RV arrives while `tourist_ally_cap` (6) recruited Canadians are with you (`canadian_allies()`). Left unhelped for 150 s, they drive off. Minimap POI "C".
- Town Hall: a `store_lots` entry ("TOWN HALL", across from the police station) dressed by `NeighborhoodBuilder._dress_town_hall()` (columns, pediment, steps, flagpole); its door is Game meta `town_hall_door`, minimap POI "T". `BribeMenu` only works within `TOWN_HALL_RANGE` (9 m) of it: the HUD line and [V] are off elsewhere, and walking away closes the menu (`BribeMenu.buy()` stays unrestricted for tests).
- `BribeMenu` ([V] at the Town Hall, then [1-3]): Municipal delay (no police/FROST next wave), Supply blockade (next wave x0.7, floored), Zoning permit (4 free barricades around the core at Phase 3 start, or immediately if bought during Phase 3). Consumed by `WaveSpawner._apply_bribes` and the level.
- Phase 1 props: `WaterMain` (now a burst fire hydrant on the sidewalk corner of the main road and the first street: bonnet knocked off, a gush and a puddle; hold [F] to cap it; group `fixables`, hold [F]: `Player._repair` falls back to fixables and `paint_jobs`), `PaintJob` (hold [F] in the ring by the house door nearest `paint_job_near`; grime patches fade), 2 `OldLady`s (`old_lady_spots`; [E] and she follows you to her green ring across the street; group `neighbors`, traffic can hit her), `Litter` (16 `litter_spots`, walk over to collect, $2 each), `ScoutPoint` (one climbable perch per datacenter outside its front-left fence corner: a treehouse (Felsa, ForProfitSI) or a railed rooftop (Scgrewgle); [E] at the ladder climbs up/down (`base_spot()`, `top_spot()`, `on_perch()`), `watch_time` on the perch marks that site's cooling units; the deed is "Scout the datacenters N/3", $50 each), `SupplyVan` (FelsaCar on a waypoint loop that hunts nobody), the `StrayDog1/2` nodes (`stray = true`: group `strays`, never attack, a treat tames them), and 10 `GrockCamera`s (Destructible poles, `grock_camera_spots` on the level: $40 and +0.02 trust each, counted on the deeds line). [E] interactables (group `interactables`: `in_reach`, `offer_text`, `interact`): `FarmersMarket` (park; [1-5] buys food that heals and crafts, all raise trust; "shop" HUD line), `SecurityCache`, `WeaponPickup` (`pickup_spots`: shovels, rock piles, a molotov crate; kinds `weapon` (any gun by `gun_name`) and `ammo` stock DUECE Hardware's free tables, `_spawn_hardware_store()`), `OldLady`. Nothing is bought except market goods and bribes. Deeds (7, each pays and raises trust; all seven pay a bonus): water main, supply van, strays, scout, grandmas, paint, litter.
- `DistrictState` (Resource): smog/noise/water_table/trust, 0..1. `EnvironmentDriver` owns the look: AgX, glow, SSAO/SSIL/SSR, SDFGI, noise-texture clouds, soft sun shadows; smog = volumetric fog (density, albedo, sun-shaft anisotropy) plus desaturation, eased toward the district state. Smog makes the day gloomy (sun 0.8), so lights switch on: `Models.smog_light()` (group `smog_lights`: street lamps, datacenter corner floodlights; no shadows, strong volumetric-fog share, distance fade) and `Models.light_cone()` fake beams (group `smog_beams`, shared translucent materials) scale with smog and are hidden below ~25%. `EnvironmentDriver.color_grade(smog)` builds a 17^3 LUT for `adjustment_color_correction` (olive, flat polluted to cool-shadow, warm-highlight restored), rebuilt when smog moves by 0.04. [F10] cycles Low/Medium/High (saved in `user://settings.cfg`); Low drops SDFGI/volumetrics/SSAO and falls back to exponential fog. Measured on an RTX 4090 laptop: High about 240 FPS, Medium about 500. `DistrictAudio` is a procedural drone (AudioStreamGenerator, Ambience bus) whose volume follows `noise`; birdsong fades in as smog drops below 0.55.
- `Destructible` (@tool StaticBody3D): builds its own box mesh/collider from `size` (origin at bottom center; `_visual_mesh()` can swap the visual).
