FPV Drone (with medium explosive)
Pistol (short range)
Shotgun (useful against defensive drones deployed by datacenter security)
Hunting Rifle (long range)
Fire hose (higher volume water, useful for attacking guards)

Level 3:
Grenades (purchased at local gun show)
Machine Guns (purchased at local gun show)
Rocket Launchers (stolen from private security caches stored onsite at datacenters)
Bulldozers & excavators (supplied by sympathetic townspeople/NPSc useful to ram fences, penetrate and destroy datacenters)

All Levels:
